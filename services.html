Services

Structural and civil engineering, independent checking, and sustainability advice, delivered online and on site in Qatar when needed.

S1

Structural design

Concept to issued-for-construction design of reinforced concrete, structural steel and composite structures: towers up to 47 storeys, hospitals, malls, industrial buildings, stadium roofs and long-span trusses.

Typical deliverables

  • Basis of design and design criteria
  • Analysis models and calculation reports
  • Issued-for-construction drawings and specifications
  • BIM-coordinated structural deliverables
  • Steel connection design with IDEA StatiCa
S2

Advanced structural analysis

Wind and earthquake loading, dynamic and nonlinear analysis, and soil-structure interaction, for structures where a standard model is not enough.

Typical deliverables

  • Response spectrum and time-history analysis, linear and nonlinear
  • Performance-based seismic design
  • Second-order (P-Delta) and stability analysis
  • Construction-stage and column-shortening analysis
  • Pile and foundation-soil interaction in 2D and 3D, with Midas GTS NX
S3

Independent verification and peer review

Third-party checks of designs prepared by others, for design teams, contractors and owners. Our principal is a certified IV&V engineer for third-party works inside Qatar Rail's exclusion and protection zones.

Peer reviews by our principal have cut construction cost by as much as 34%.

Typical deliverables

  • Review of concept, criteria, models and calculations
  • Independent checks of analysis models
  • Compliance reviews for Qatar Rail zone developments
  • Construction inspection by an independent engineer
  • Comments register and close-out
S4

Forensic investigation and rehabilitation

We find the cause of cracking, deflection, settlement and deterioration, then design the repair. We also assess existing structures for a new use or new equipment.

Typical deliverables

  • Structural condition assessment
  • Root-cause investigation: cracking, deflection, differential settlement, construction defects, overload, earthquake damage, flooding, durability
  • Strengthening and retrofit design
  • Change-of-use checks, such as new MRI and CT equipment or an office-to-hotel conversion
  • Remaining service-life assessment
S5

Value engineering

Lower construction cost without lowering safety or code compliance. We review structural systems, column layouts and foundations early, when changes cost least.

Typical deliverables

  • Techno-economic comparison of structural systems
  • Foundation and pile optimisation
  • Column layout coordination with the architect
  • Work-in-progress design review workshops
  • Comparative cost analysis
S6

Construction-phase support

Fast, precise answers for contractors and supervision teams, so site work does not wait on the design.

Typical deliverables

  • Shop drawing and material submittal review
  • Requests for information and site queries
  • Method statements and inspection and test plans
  • Alternative designs proposed by contractors
  • Lifting-point and transport analysis for large steel elements
S7

Civil and infrastructure works

Design support for the civil works that serve buildings and sites, and for stand-alone infrastructure.

Typical deliverables

  • Roads and bridges
  • Drainage and water works
  • Pumping stations, tunnels and underground structures
  • Supports and anchors for pressurised piping
  • Slope protection and flood control
S8

Sustainability and GSAS

GSAS assessment and reporting led by a GSAS Design & Build Certified Green Professional, so green targets are set while the design can still change.

Typical deliverables

  • Sustainability assessment and reporting
  • Early-stage green building strategy
  • Review of the design against GSAS criteria
  • Coordination of evidence with the design team
